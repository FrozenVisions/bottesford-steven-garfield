People often wait before they write to me. There might be only a few boxes. The sleeves might be scuffed, and the records played. Or the house might be in Nottingham or Melton Mowbray, and it feels awkward to ask someone in Bottesford to make the journey. If you are clearing a house, moving to somewhere smaller, or sorting records a parent or relative left behind, you can write anyway.

I am Steven Gee. I live in Bottesford, NG13, in the Vale of Belvoir. Pink Moon Records is just me. There is no shop, and this is not a business. I buy vinyl for my own shelves. I will come and see your records in the comfort of your own home.

## A few records is reason enough

Anywhere nearby, a few records are worth an email. I do not need a room lined with shelves before I will come. A short row on a bookcase, a crate in the spare room, or two boxes from a loft is a perfectly ordinary way for records to turn up when a house is being emptied. Any situation or quantity is always considered.

Downsizing is often how those boxes appear. The smaller place will not take the old shelves, and your records are what get left until last. The same happens when you have inherited them and never meant to own a stack of vinyl. You do not have to learn the names on the labels. Say they were a relative's, or that they came with the house, if that helps. Or simply say there are boxes and you would like me to see them.

If you are in [Bottesford](/), or in a Vale village such as [Muston](/sell-vinyl-records/muston/), [Redmile](/sell-vinyl-records/redmile/), [Orston](/sell-vinyl-records/orston/) or [Belvoir](/sell-vinyl-records/belvoir/), tell me where you are and I will come. The same is true in [Bingham](/sell-vinyl-records/bingham/), [Grantham](/sell-vinyl-records/grantham/) and [Newark](/sell-vinyl-records/newark-on-trent/). I would rather view your records where they are than have them posted to me. A short note is enough to start: "I want to sell my records", and the name of the place.

## Further away, when the records are worth the journey

For a good collection I will travel any distance. Write from Nottingham, Melton Mowbray, or wherever the house is. If you are clearing a family home there, or your records will not fit the place you are moving to, name the town and I will come and see them.

The villages along the way are just as welcome. [Radcliffe-on-Trent](/sell-vinyl-records/radcliffe-on-trent/) is on the way towards Nottingham. [Long Clawson](/sell-vinyl-records/long-clawson/) is out towards Melton Mowbray. If your records are in either place, or in a village close by, say so.

## Played copies are welcome

People also apologise for the condition. You can leave that out. Original UK pressings, gatefold sleeves and the old label designs make me smile. Island pink label, Vertigo swirl, Harvest, Charisma, Deram, Atlantic plum, Parlophone and Immediate are names I like to see. Plain, played, well-loved copies are welcome too. A record that has been on a turntable for years is still a record I want to hold.

The records wanted first are late 1960s and early 1970s rock, and I mean any type of it: psychedelic, blues rock, hard rock, progressive, folk rock, heavy rock, early heavy metal, garage and freakbeat. Albums, singles and box sets, in their sleeves if you still have them. A box of singles from those years interests me as much as a shelf of albums.

A house being emptied is seldom only rock. Tell me about all of your records. I collect all kinds of music for my own shelves: soul and Motown, country, folk, reggae, and mixed boxes from the loft. A good record is never turned away. If you want to sell vinyl records and you are not sure they are my sort, send the rough idea anyway. I am a record buyer, and I would rather see them than have you decide for me.
