I am Steven Garfield. I live in Bottesford, NG13, in the Vale of Belvoir, and I buy vinyl record collections. Some people know me as Steven Gee, which is how I write my name on my card at Bottesford VC Hall. It is the same man.

## How far I will travel

Ten miles, in a straight line from the middle of Bottesford, and no further. That takes in Bingham, Grantham, Newark-on-Trent, Radcliffe-on-Trent and the villages of the Vale of Belvoir. Melton Mowbray, Ancaster, Southwell and Nottingham are outside my round.

## I come to you

I would rather sit with the records in your house than have them posted to me. Tell me which village you are in and I will come and look at them.

## What I am mainly looking for

Rock from the 1960s and 1970s is the music I have loved longest, and I mean any type of it: psychedelic, blues rock, hard rock, progressive, folk rock, heavy rock, early heavy metal, garage and freakbeat. Bands such as Led Zeppelin, Deep Purple, Black Sabbath, Pink Floyd, The Who, Cream, Jimi Hendrix, The Doors, Fleetwood Mac, Free, Traffic, Jethro Tull, King Crimson, Yes, Genesis, the Rolling Stones, The Kinks, Small Faces, Steppenwolf and Santana. Labels such as Island pink label, Vertigo swirl, Harvest, Charisma, Deram, Atlantic plum, Parlophone and Immediate.

## Records that are not rock

I will still look. I collect for my own shelves, and I am glad of anything that belongs there: jazz, classical, soul and Motown, country, folk, reggae, film soundtracks, and mixed boxes from the loft. Tell me about the whole collection, not only the rock.
