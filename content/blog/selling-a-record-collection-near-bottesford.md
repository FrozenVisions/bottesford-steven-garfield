I am Steven Gee. I live in Bottesford, NG13, in the Vale of Belvoir, and I buy vinyl record collections.

## How far I will travel

Up to 30 miles from my home in Bottesford, or even further if you have a very large or unusual collection.

## I come to you

I will come and view your records in the comfort of your own home. Any situation or quantity is always considered. Tell me where you are and I will come and look at them.

## What I am mainly looking for

Rock from the 1960s and 1970s is the music I have loved longest, and I mean any type of it: psychedelic, blues rock, hard rock, progressive, folk rock, heavy rock, early heavy metal, garage and freakbeat. Labels such as Island pink label, Vertigo swirl, Harvest, Charisma, Deram, Atlantic plum, Parlophone and Immediate.

## Records that are not rock

That is OK. I still may be interested in buying it, as I collect all kinds of music for my own shelves: jazz, classical, soul and Motown, country, folk, reggae, film soundtracks, and mixed boxes from the loft. Tell me about the whole collection, not only the rock.
