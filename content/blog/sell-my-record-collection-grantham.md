[Grantham](/sell-vinyl-records/grantham/) is in Lincolnshire, near Manthorpe. Tell me the records are there, and I will come and view them at your home.

## Before you sell my record collection Grantham

There is no record shop to bring them to. I come to you. I would rather view your vinyl records where they are than have them posted to me.

You do not need a full list. A rough idea of what is in the collection is enough, or a few photos attached to the email. I can come round and look. Any situation or quantity always considered.

A few boxes in Grantham that have not been played for years are reason enough to write. Email me before they go to the tip. The same is true if you are clearing a house, moving to somewhere smaller, or sorting vinyl records a parent or relative left behind. You do not have to learn the names on the labels.

## Vinyl records I hope to find

Rock from the 1960s and 1970s is the music I have loved longest, and I mean any type of it: psychedelic, blues rock, hard rock, progressive, folk rock, heavy rock, early heavy metal, garage and freakbeat. The vinyl records wanted first are late 1960s and early 1970s rock. Albums, singles and box sets, in their sleeves if you still have them.

Original UK pressings, gatefold sleeves and the old label designs make me smile. Island pink label, Vertigo swirl, Harvest, Charisma, Deram, Atlantic plum, Parlophone and Immediate are names I like to see. I will look at plain, played, well-loved copies too.

Tell me about the whole collection, not only the rock. I collect all kinds of music for my own shelves: soul and Motown, country, folk, reggae, and mixed boxes from the loft. A good record is never turned away.
